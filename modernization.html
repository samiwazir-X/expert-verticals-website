Lifecycle Upgrades

When the Building Evolves, the Elevator Should Too.

Aging elevator systems often suffer from frequent breakdowns, obsolete electrical parts, jerky ride quality, and high energy bills. Modernization transforms safety, performance, and property value without the cost of full shaft reconstruction.

TIER 01 · CONTROLLER & DRIVE

Microprocessor & VVVF Upgrades

Replace energy-inefficient relay logic controllers with solid-state microprocessor panels and variable frequency (VVVF) inverters. Delivers silky smooth acceleration, pinpoint leveling, and up to 40% reduction in electricity consumption.

Enquire on Controller Upgrades
TIER 02 · DOOR SYSTEMS

Door Operator & Safety Modernization

Modernize noisy, slow door operators with high-torque VVVF door motors, multi-beam infrared door safety curtains, and certified electromechanical landing door locks to eliminate the #1 cause of elevator shutdowns.

Enquire on Door Modernization
TIER 03 · AESTHETICS

Cabin Refurbishment & Fixtures

Upgrade worn interiors with hairline / mirror stainless steel wall panels, architectural LED ambient ceiling lights, granite / anti-slip PVC flooring, stainless handrails, and modern digital Car Operating Panels (COP).

Enquire on Cabin Refurbishment
TIER 04 · SAFETY & RESCUE

Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD)

Equip elevators with Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD) that automatically move the elevator to the nearest floor and open doors during utility power grid outages, ensuring passenger peace of mind.

Enquire on ARD Installation
