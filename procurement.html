Submit your Bill of Quantities (BOQ), architectural hoistway drawings, or tender specifications. Our estimation and engineering team will provide a line-item quotation and technical compliance review within 48 business hours.

Procurement Guidelines

What to Include for a Rapid Technical Response.

To expedite accurate engineering sizing and pricing, please ensure your submission includes:

  • Building type, location, and total floor count / travel height
  • Available shaft width × depth dimensions and pit/overhead heights
  • Required capacity (kg / persons) and operating speed (m/s)
  • Cabin finishes (Hairline SS, Mirror, Glass, Panoramic)
  • Project stage, tender submission deadline & execution timeline
