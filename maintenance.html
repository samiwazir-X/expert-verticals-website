Preventive Maintenance Standard

What Happens During Our Maintenance Inspection?.

Every scheduled maintenance visit follows an engineering checklist to ensure all electromechanical systems are verified, tested, adjusted, and certified for smooth operation.

Request Maintenance Quote
  • Machine traction sheave & gearbox oil check
  • Electromagnetic brake clearance & torque test
  • Overspeed governor & safety gear verification
  • Landing door interlock contacts & closers
  • Infrared multi-beam door safety curtain test
  • Traveling cable & electrical terminal check
  • Guide rail lubrication & roller shoe alignment
  • Pit buffer clearance & emergency stop switch
  • Floor leveling accuracy calibration
  • Emergency car lighting, alarm & intercom test

PASSENGER SAFETY PROTOCOL

Emergency Entrapment Protocol.

If an elevator experiences an unexpected power outage or shutdown with passengers inside: Contact authorized building technicians or our support desk immediately. Never attempt to pry elevator doors open from inside or outside.
