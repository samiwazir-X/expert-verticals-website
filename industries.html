Industry Sectors

Different Buildings. Unique Passenger Dynamics.

A hospital elevator requires emergency medical priority; a corporate headquarters requires high-speed peak-interval dispatching; a factory lift requires brutal structural load capacity. We engineer each system to its specific sector reality.

Customized Engineering for Every Operating Environment.

SEC/01 · RESIDENTIAL

Residential & High-Rise Towers

Apartment towers, housing schemes, high-rise condominiums, and private villas. Focus on smooth acceleration, quiet cabin acoustics, low energy standby modes, and family-safe door curtain sensors.

  • Traffic PatternTwo-way morning departures & evening arrivals
  • Key PrioritiesAcoustic isolation, low vibration, child safety
  • Recommended SystemGearless MRL Passenger Lifts / Home Lifts

Corporate headquarters, financial centres, IT parks, and multi-tenant commercial complexes. Designed for high passenger turnover with destination dispatching and smart turnstile integrations.

  • Traffic PatternIntense 5-minute peak morning & lunch rushes

Hospitals & Medical Facilities

Specialized medical clinics, tertiary care hospitals, and surgical centres. Extra-deep cabin dimensions, Code Blue priority override, micro-leveling for stretcher transfers, and easy-clean stainless finishes.

  • Traffic Pattern24/7 continuous patient, visitor & supply transit
  • Key PrioritiesZero jolt, exact floor alignment, sterile hygiene
  • Recommended SystemBed / Hospital Elevators with Priority Dispatch
