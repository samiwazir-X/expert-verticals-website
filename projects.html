Project Quality & Governance

Engineering Movement. Documented with Discipline.

We believe in transparent engineering, rigorous site verification, and factual representation. Discover how our quality management framework governs every vertical mobility installation.

05Execution Standards

Our 5-Stage Project Quality Management Protocol.

GATE 01 · SHAFT AUDIT

1. Structural Shaft Verification

Before equipment mobilization, our engineers verify shaft vertical plumb alignment, pit depth, overhead headroom clearance, wall beam anchoring points, and ventilation apertures against approved architectural drawings.

GATE 02 · MATERIAL INSPECTION

2. Equipment Receipt & Submittals

Complete uncrating inspection of traction machines, guide rails, VVVF drive panels, traveling cables, and cabin components with serial numbers logged against technical data submittals.

GATE 03 · PRECISION ERECTION

3. Guide Rail Plumb & Bracket Alignment

Guide rail brackets are laser-aligned and torqued to exact engineering tolerances. Flawless rail alignment prevents lateral vibration, delivering whisper-quiet, silky ride dynamics.

GATE 04 · SAFETY COMMISSIONING

4. Load & Safety Gear Drop Tests

Full 125% rated load testing, governor overspeed safety gear tripping, buffer compression checks, phase failure trips, and emergency power backup simulations before client inspection.

GATE 05 · HANDOVER & LOGS

5. Handover Dossier & Training

Delivery of comprehensive As-Built electrical schematics, operating manuals, maintenance schedules, key handover, and safety training for building facility staff.

VERIFIED CLIENT CREDENTIALS

Procurement & Reference Dossiers.

Procurement committees and corporate clients may request verified reference letters, equipment specifications, and SECP/PEC registration context directly from our corporate procurement desk.
