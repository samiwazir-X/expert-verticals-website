1. Executive Summary & Scope

Expert Verticals delivers comprehensive vertical mobility solutions across Pakistan. Operating as an engineering-led business vertical of The Expert, we serve developers, architects, general contractors, healthcare networks, industrial plants, and public-sector institutions.

Our operational capabilities span the entire elevator lifecycle: design-stage hoistway coordination, equipment sourcing, mechanical and electrical installation, testing and commissioning, emergency maintenance, and system modernization.

2. Core Vertical Mobility Systems

Passenger Elevators

Gearless MRL and machine-room elevators (450 kg – 2,000 kg, speeds 1.0 – 4.0 m/s).

Hospital & Bed Lifts

Bed and stretcher dimensions (1,600 kg – 2,500 kg) with direct landing control and Code Blue priority.

Industrial Freight Lifts

Heavy-duty cargo lifts (1,000 kg – 10,000+ kg) with reinforced flooring and bi-parting doors.

Panoramic Glass Lifts

Custom architectural transparent glass installations for malls, hotels, and luxury atriums.

Home & Villa Lifts

Compact, whisper-quiet private residence lifts with minimal pit and headroom requirements.

Dumbwaiters & Platform Lifts

Food & pharmaceutical transport (50–300 kg) and barrier-free platform mobility systems.
