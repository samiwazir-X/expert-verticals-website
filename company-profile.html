1. Executive Summary & Scope

Expert Verticals coordinates two critical building systems across Pakistan: elevators and vertical mobility, plus fire fighting and life safety. We support developers, architects, contractors, healthcare facilities, industrial operators and public-sector institutions.

Our work spans requirement assessment, engineering coordination, equipment sourcing, installation support, testing, commissioning and planned lifecycle maintenance.
